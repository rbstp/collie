# Dictation and attachments

## Dictation

The mic button dictates into the prompt field with Apple's on-device speech models, in English (US) or French (Canada). The audio never leaves the phone. While you speak, a waveform fills the dictation bar so you can see quiet speech and pauses, and the language and stop controls stay within reach.

Dictated text stays in the prompt field until you send it.

## Attachments

Use the paperclip to attach up to 10 photos or files to a prompt. They upload over the tailnet and show as thumbnails you can remove before sending. The agent receives their paths on the machine.

On the machine, attachments are capped at 20 MiB per file and 200 MiB in total, stored in a private folder, and deleted after 24 hours.
