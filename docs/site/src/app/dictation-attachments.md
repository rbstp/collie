# Dictation and attachments

## Dictation

Tap the waveform button to dictate in English (US) or French (Canada); long-press it to pick the language. While you dictate, tap **EN** or **FR** to switch, and the waveform again to stop. Apple's on-device speech models transcribe your speech, so the audio never leaves the phone.

To start over in an agent's prompt field without stopping, tap the clear button twice: once to turn it red, then again to empty the field and listen again. When you are not dictating, one tap clears the field.

## Attachments

Use the paperclip to attach up to 10 photos or files to a prompt; remove any before sending. They upload over the tailnet, and the agent receives their paths on the machine. Each file can be up to 20 MiB, and a machine holds up to 200 MiB of attachments. `collied` stores them in a private folder and deletes them after 24 hours.
