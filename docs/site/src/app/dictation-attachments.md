# Dictation and attachments

## Dictation

Tap the waveform button to dictate into the prompt field, in English (US) or French (Canada); long-press it to pick the language. While you dictate, tap **EN** or **FR** to switch language, and the waveform again to stop. Apple's on-device speech models transcribe it, so the audio never leaves the phone. Dictated text stays in the prompt field until you send it.

To start over in an agent's prompt field without stopping, tap the clear button twice. The first tap turns it red; the second empties the field and starts listening again after a brief pause. The button turns back after a few seconds, when new words arrive or when dictation stops, so a later single tap never clears by surprise. When you are not dictating, one tap clears the field.

## Attachments

Use the paperclip to attach up to 10 photos or files to a prompt. They upload over the tailnet and show as thumbnails, which you can remove before sending. The agent receives their paths on the machine.

Each file can be up to 20 MiB, and a machine holds up to 200 MiB of attachments in total. `collied` stores them in a private folder and deletes them after 24 hours.
