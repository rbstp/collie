# Dictation and attachments

## Dictation

Tap the mic button to dictate into the prompt field, in English (US) or French (Canada). Apple's on-device speech models transcribe it, so the audio never leaves the phone. While you speak, a waveform in the dictation bar shows quiet speech and pauses, and the language and stop controls stay within reach.

Dictated text stays in the prompt field until you send it.

To start over without stopping, tap the clear button twice. The first tap turns it red, and the second empties the field while dictation keeps listening. The red button goes back to normal after a few seconds, when new words arrive, or when dictation stops, so a later single tap never clears by surprise. When you are not dictating, one tap clears the field.

## Attachments

Use the paperclip to attach up to 10 photos or files to a prompt. They upload over the tailnet and show as thumbnails, which you can remove before sending. The agent receives their paths on the machine.

Each file can be up to 20 MiB, and all attachments on a machine up to 200 MiB in total. `collied` stores them in a private folder and deletes them after 24 hours.
