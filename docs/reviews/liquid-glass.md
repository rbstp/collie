Issue #207 uses the iOS 26 and watchOS 26 glass APIs directly; the minimum versions stay at 26. System navigation, sheets, menus, pickers and search keep their native styling.

Standalone terminal keys, attachments, dictation, send, keyboard dismissal and attachment removal use native glass button styles. The shared attachment button keeps its native menu stable while open and presents Saved prompts, Files or Photo Library after dismissal completes. Its 16-point paperclip uses the chosen app accent, its glass circle matches the 38-point dictation, keyboard and send circles, and its 44-point hit target extends past the circle. Picker presentation is local and does not wait for the machine; the upload chip distinguishes preparing a file, waiting for the machine and upload progress. The wrap-lines control is a toolbar button on the shared glass capsule, its icon in the accent when on and the primary color when off, like the other glass toolbar icons; a button-style toggle would fill the bar item. Its title carries the state (Wrap lines on or off), because bar buttons pass only their title to accessibility.

Chat/Terminal is a compact icon-only selector in the session header row, with 44-point segments and the VoiceOver labels Chat and Terminal. It has no glass backing: an extra glass layer beside other glass left keyboard animations unfinished after a picker closed. Every segmented control (Agents/Usage, the inbox filters, the Usage costs range and Chat/Terminal) uses accent-colored selected text and the system's own selection, with no forced black or white selected segment; the system draws the selection darker in the toolbar (Agents/Usage) than in content. In a toolbar, UIKit leaves a tapped segmented control's press lens over its selection, keeping the selected label in its pressed tint; once the lens settles, the control is re-added to its window, which restores the accent, and keeps its frame. Segmented controls drop UIKit's one-point bottom alignment inset, which they draw over, so Agents/Usage matches the height of the glass capsule beside it. Agents/Usage uses native interactive page scrolling instead of a gesture that switches only after release, and page transitions triggered by the selector respect Reduce Motion.

Approval disclosure uses a native button. Adjacent custom glass controls share `GlassEffectContainer`. Controls inside a glass composer or dictation capsule add no further glass surface. The accent-colored dictation waveform uses a translucent gradient that becomes solid with Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast. It fills the available capsule width with recent microphone levels, including repeated levels and silence, on a more sensitive microphone level scale; its history clears when dictation stops or switches language.

Reference: [Apple's Liquid Glass guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/applying-liquid-glass-to-custom-views).

The follow-up control audit found native `List`, `NavigationLink` and `Gauge` views throughout the watch app and widgets. Watch Approve uses `.glassProminent`; Deny and answer choices use `.glass`. No watch control needed replacing.

## Validation

Prioritize dark mode while keeping native appearance and accessibility behavior. Glass uses the system material and respects the user's glass setting.

- `just ios-test`: passed, including 274 app tests and 62 Ghostty tests.
- `just lint`: passed. The later Codex prompt reflow change also passed all 334 `collied` tests, targeted Clippy and formatting checks; its regression test fails before the fix and passes after.
- Signed iPhone build, embedded watch app and widgets: built and passed signature verification.
- Reviewed the final diff for approval guards, disabled states, composer behavior and widget missing and stale states. Fixed clipped terminal key labels during visual review. Physical watch feedback exposed a widget-only collapse in the geometry-scaled gauges; the update removes that transform and shares the plain native gauge with usage detail.
- Simulator fixtures covered light, dark, Increase Contrast, accessibility text sizes and actual Reduce Transparency. Verified native paging at a partial offset with both pages visible and after settling on Usage. Approval actions stack at accessibility sizes. Existing agent header and key clipping at extreme text sizes remains outside this change.
- Dictation fixtures cover dark and light appearance, a narrow bar and accessibility text with Increase Contrast. Session tests verify speech and silence history, its size bound, and clearing on stop, cancellation and language change. The user accepted the latest phone build after the microphone sensitivity adjustment.
- Native watch simulator previews and rectangular fixtures cover reset countdowns and missing data. Standalone previews did not reproduce the physical widget layout failure; physical feedback confirmed the collapse was resolved and the compact usage list looks good. The widget now dims its unused arc and uses bold countdowns; the user accepted these final refinements on the physical watch.

These screenshots use deterministic fixtures rendered with production views. The watch simulator image uses a temporary host app with the production gauge view, not a configured watch face.
